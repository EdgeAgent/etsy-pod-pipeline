# Appendix — Creating a Shop From Scratch (optional reference)

**Read this only if the Stage 1 intake says you don't have a shop
yet.** The pipeline assumes an existing shop; this appendix is the
one-time detour that creates it. It is a human's-hands walkthrough:
Etsy shop creation touches identity, banking, and tax information —
those steps are **hands-only, always**. The agent walks the human
through it tap by tap, prepares everything preparable in advance,
and never touches a credential.

> State this was written for: an **Etsy buyer account already
> exists** (signed in), but **no shop has been created yet**.

## Before you tap anything — prepare (agent can help)

- [ ] **Shop name** chosen and checked for availability (Etsy checks as
      you type during setup; have 2–3 fallbacks ready). The name is
      your brand on every package and search result.
- [ ] **Shop language, country, currency** decided. A Canadian shop
      selling mostly to Canada/US buyers usually lists in CAD with a
      CAD payment account (listing in a different currency than your
      payment account costs a 2.5% currency-conversion fee per sale).
- [ ] **First listing** ready to draft during setup — Etsy walks you
      through creating your first listing as part of opening the shop.
      Have one product roughed out, or create the shop with a simple
      first product and rebuild listings properly at Stage 7.
- [ ] **Bank account details** at hand (institution/transit/account
      numbers) — for Etsy Payments deposits. Human enters these.
- [ ] **Government photo ID** at hand — Etsy Payments identity
      verification can require it. Human only; never photographed,
      uploaded, or handled by the agent.
- [ ] **Tax information** at hand — for a Canadian sole proprietor:
      legal name, address, and SIN (or business number if registered);
      plus your GST/HST situation (registration is generally required
      once worldwide taxable sales pass C$30,000 over four consecutive
      calendar quarters — confirm your own situation; this is general
      information, not tax advice). Human enters all of it.

## The walkthrough (human's hands, agent guiding)

1. Sign in to the existing Etsy account → **Sell on Etsy** →
   **Open your Etsy shop**.
2. Set shop preferences: language, country (Canada), currency (CAD).
3. Name the shop (Etsy checks availability live).
4. Create the first listing when prompted (photos can be Printify
   mockups later — for setup, complete the flow; you will rebuild
   listings properly at Stage 7).
5. **Set-up fee**: Etsy may charge a **one-time, non-refundable shop
   set-up fee**. The amount varies by location and is **shown during
   setup before you confirm** — there is no fixed public price. If it
   appears, the human decides and pays it on their own card. This is
   real money out before a single sale; budget for it.
6. Set your **billing method** (the card Etsy charges fees to if your
   sales balance doesn't cover them).
7. Enrol in **Etsy Payments** (mandatory to sell): enter bank account
   details and complete **identity verification**. This step can pause
   for Etsy's review — that is normal.
8. Tax step: enter the tax information Etsy asks for. Canadian sellers
   must include any seller-responsible taxes in their listing prices —
   the pricing calculator (Stage 5) works on tax-inclusive thinking.
9. Open the shop. **Do not publish real listings yet** — that is
   Stage 7, behind the publish gate. Return to Stage 1's health
   check and run it — a brand-new shop should pass it trivially.

## Hard rules for this appendix

- The agent **never** enters, receives, stores, or transmits: passwords,
  banking details, government ID numbers, or tax IDs. If a step needs
  one, the human types it, on their own device, while the agent waits.
- Identity verification is the human's step even if it is slow or
  annoying. There is no workaround and the agent will not look for one.
- Bank account must be added within 30 days of the first sale and
  verified within 60 days, or Etsy can suspend the shop — set it up
  during onboarding, not after the first order.
