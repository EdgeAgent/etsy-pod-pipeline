## The fee stack

| Fee | Rate | Charged on | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shop set-up fee | One-time; **amount varies by location, shown during setup**; non-refundable | Shop opening, if Etsy requires it | Fees & Payments Policy |
| Listing fee | **US$0.20 per listing** | On publish and each 4-month renewal; per extra quantity sold on multi-quantity listings; charged whether or not the item sells | Fees & Payments Policy |
| Transaction fee | **6.5%** | Displayed price + shipping + gift wrap (+ paid personalisation). Non-US sellers: also on seller-responsible tax included in the price | Fees & Payments Policy |
| Regulatory operating fee — **Canada** | **0.50%** | Same base as the transaction fee | Etsy Help, Regulatory Operating Fee (Canada listed at 0.50%) |
| Payment processing — **Canada** | **3% + C$0.25** per order (buyers in Canada or the US); **4% + C$0.25** (other international buyers) | Total sale amount incl. shipping and tax | Etsy Help, Payment Processing Fees |
| Offsite Ads fee | **15%** (shops under US$10,000 trailing-365-day sales; may opt out) / **12%** (at/above threshold, mandatory, lifetime) | Attributed orders only; capped at **US$100 per order** | Fees & Payments Policy |
| Currency conversion | **2.5%** | When listing currency ≠ payment-account currency | Fees & Payments Policy |
| Etsy Plus (optional) | US$10/month | Subscription | Fees & Payments Policy |

## Notes that change real pricing

- **Fees are charged on shipping too.** Moving $6 from "shipping"
  into the item price does not reduce the transaction fee — the base
  is the same. (Fees & Payments Policy.)
- **GST/HST on Etsy's fees:** Etsy charges Canadian sales tax on its
  service fees (rate by province — 13% HST in Ontario) unless the
  seller's tax status on file says otherwise. This sits ON TOP of the
  table above and is easy to miss in margin math. *(Reported
  consistently by Canadian seller resources citing Etsy's Help pages;
  confirm in your own Payment account after your first fees post —
  this is the one figure in this doc not quoted from a fetched
  official page.)*
- **Deposits:** Etsy Payments deposit schedules (daily/weekly/
  biweekly/monthly) are chosen in the Payment account. Sellers
  outside the US must add a bank account within 30 days of the first
  sale and verify it within 60 days or the shop can be suspended.
- **The set-up fee has no published amount.** Anyone quoting you a
  fixed figure is guessing. The number that matters is the one Etsy
  shows during YOUR setup, before you confirm.
